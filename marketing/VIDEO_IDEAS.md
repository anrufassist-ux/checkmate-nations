# Video ideas (backlog)

Evergreen clips besides the daily news satire. All vertical, 15–35 s, English text, end card "Play free now". Status: idea / ready / posted.
Made with `tools/clip/` (scripted game, camera moves, UI clicks via Playwright). Post texts go to `POSTS.md` once a clip exists.

## Series "Meet the nations" (one clip per nation, 15–20 s)
Slow orbit close around the nation's pieces, then a name card. Welcome tone, no mockery of the country.
- Card: flag, "Welcome, <nation>!", the king (caricature) and the queen side by side.
- Close-ups: pawn, tank, drone, jet in the nation's colours, queen last.
- Text: "<nation> joins Checkmate Nations 🇫🇷 Who should they play first? 👇"
- Order: France (new) first, then India, UK, Brazil, the rest.

## Series "Queen profile" (Steckbrief, 20–30 s)
Close-up turntable of the queen, 3–4 short fact cards. Historic queens with dignity, no jokes.
- India: Rani Lakshmibai, Queen of Jhansi, rode into battle in 1857–58, symbol of the Indian independence movement.
- UK: Queen Elizabeth II, 70 years on the throne (1952–2022), longest reign of any British monarch.
- Brazil: Empress Leopoldina (1797–1826), Austrian-born, signed the decree that led to Brazil's independence in 1822.
- Symbols with a wink: Statue of Liberty (USA), Matryoshka (Russia), panda (China), Europa (EU), German shepherd with beer (Germany), Marianne (France), hoopoe (Israel's national bird), Persian cat (Iran).
- Check every fact again before the clip.
